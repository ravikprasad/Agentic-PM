Project management certification: required or desired?

By Ravi Kaushal Prasad

Looking back, I remember standing at a career crossroads, wondering whether a certification was the right next step. Like many of you, I felt overwhelmed by the options; each one promising a clear path forward, a higher salary, and a place among a network of certified professionals. In addition to that, LinkedIn is flooded with professionals boasting such certifications.

As a project and program management practitioner, I began with one simple question: why? I was already succeeding in my role as a project manager and practicing project management in day-to-day work, so what would a certification really add was the most burning question around me? To find out, I looked around and spoke with my mentors & seniors and with leaders in my organization whom I admired. Their answers showed me that there is a thin line between a certification being required vs desired. The clearest difference appeared in consulting roles and in the entry- to mid-level roles, where experience alone is often not enough and a certification can play a vital role in setting you apart. If you are weighing a certification right now, I encourage you to start with the same question: why?
