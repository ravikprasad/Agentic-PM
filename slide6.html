AI Fundamentals

Let's decipher AI together

Artificial Intelligence

  • Technique that enables machines to mimic human intelligence.
  • Example: YouTube, Facebook, Alexa, Netflix, autonomous cars.

Machine Learning

  • Advanced algorithms detect patterns in large data sets to learn and adapt.
  • Example: social media, search and ads, fraud detection.

Neural Network

  • Subset of ML: layered, interconnected nodes replicate the human brain and improve over time.
  • Example: face recognition, object detection for autonomous systems.

Deep Learning

  • Built on neural networks; trains itself for in-depth data processing and analytics.
  • Example: digital voice assistants such as Siri or Alexa.

Generative AI

  • Trained on vast datasets to generate text, code, or images.
  • Example: ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, chatbots.

Natural Language Processing

  • Ability to understand, process, and generate human language (text or voice).
