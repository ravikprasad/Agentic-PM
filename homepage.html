01 About

Human-centered technical leadership.
Engineering meaningful impact.

With 20+ years in technology, I build programs that connect application security, developer productivity and customer outcomes.

At GitHub, I lead global enterprise programs for developer platform migrations, securing applications using GitHub Advanced Security and CodeQL. I run multi-million-dollar application security engagements covering SAST assessments, training and enterprise wide adoption. I've also delivered C/C++ coding standards programs for automotive customers aligned to MISRA 2023, AUTOSAR and ISO 26262.

At Microsoft, I played diverse customer facing roles from Product Support Engineering to Principal Technical Account Manager. In my last role as a Technical Account Manager for advertising partners generating $900M+ in annual ad revenue. I delivered 12+ feature adoption programs that added more than $20M in partner revenue. Before that, I led a $100M+ Azure IoT connected-car program with an 85+ member team, reaching 95+% customer satisfaction and a multi-year contract renewal. My career began with SharePoint architecture support and dev leadership, which gives me a hands-on engineering foundation. Today I use it to connect engineering, product, sales and services teams, turn strategy into execution and earn customers' trust.

I hold PMP, Certified ScrumMaster, ITIL v3, Prosci Change Management, GitHub Foundations and GitHub Copilot certifications, plus Cornell certificates in Cybersecurity and Systems Design.

As Director of Academic Initiatives at Puget Sound PMI, I mentor aspiring project managers and share practical approaches to project management, DevOps, agile delivery and cloud technology.
